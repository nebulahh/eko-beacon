# Product taste

Eko Beacon should feel like a thoughtful tabletop puzzle placed on a real rooftop. It offers a short, satisfying signal-routing challenge and gives each player a visible place in the scene through their start.ng character.

## Principles

1. **Distinct play.** Solve a spatial light-routing puzzle; avoid another Lagos runner, traffic dodger, or market collection game.
2. **Identity matters.** The player's centrally styled start.ng avatar is a visible rooftop companion, not a generic placeholder.
3. **Readable cause and effect.** Every mirror turn immediately updates the beam path. Players can see why a route succeeds, stops, or loops.
4. **Quick understanding.** The goal and controls are apparent at first glance; levels grow gently more complex.
5. **Platform-native accounts.** Use start.ng's identity, guest, sign-in, and leaderboard bridge. Never collect credentials or create a separate account.

## Quality bar

- A guest can play immediately and sees the supplied Guest character.
- A signed-in player sees their name, account, and styled character.
- The first puzzle can be solved in a few deliberate mirror turns.
- Players can solve multiple levels and restart without reloading.
- Completed signed-in runs save to start.ng; guests receive a clear sign-in prompt.
- The game works on mouse, keyboard, and touch, with no dead controls or placeholder states.
